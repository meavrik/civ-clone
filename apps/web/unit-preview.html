Driven by the game's own UnitAnimator on a 2.5 s cycle: a fight starts each cycle and lasts 1.6 s, the attack going round and round (its first strike timed to land with the first blow, 150 ms in), the sentry loops, the fortifier is ordered to fortify, then stands again; the traveller walks for 1.6 s, then stands; each job loops. Placement is the game's unitArtPlace: the cyan cross is the tile anchor, the grey box the frame canvas, the yellow box the drawing.
